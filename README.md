# Product Analytics & Internship Learning

This repository documents the concepts, research, and practical experience gained during my Product Technology internship at Trademo.

Rather than focusing on a single project, it serves as a collection of notes and documentation covering analytics engineering, product thinking, trade intelligence, and dashboarding.

## Repository Structure

- **Event tracking and amplitude.md**  
  Covers event taxonomy, analytics instrumentation, SDK integration, dashboards, cohorts, segmentation, and product analytics concepts using Amplitude.

- **Grafana.md**  
  Documents dashboard creation, product metrics, monitoring, visualization, and analytics reporting.

- **Learning.md**  
  Consolidated notes on product development, trade intelligence, competitor research, enterprise engineering practices, AI applications, risk analysis, supply chain intelligence, and related topics explored during the internship.
